Redefining the Future of Packaging.

Founded on the belief that packaging shouldn't outlive the product it protects, EcoPack has been pioneering compostable and biodegradable materials for over a decade.

Our Mission

To accelerate the world's transition to zero-waste commerce by providing functional, beautiful, and earth-friendly packaging alternatives to every business, regardless of size.

Our Vision

A circular economy where every piece of packaging is reused, recycled, or returned to the earth rapidly without leaving any toxic residue or microplastics behind.

Our Journey

2010

Company Founded

2016
